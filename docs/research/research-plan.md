# Private Meet competitor research plan

Date: 2026-10-10
POC: Project owner
TL;DR: Compare the products people would combine to replace Private Meet: scheduling, private calls, social introductions, and casual poker.

## Questions

- Which alternatives cover booking, guest joining, calls, and shared play in one flow?
- What are their free limits, paid units, seat requirements, and pricing qualifications?
- Where are media, identities, booking information, chat, and game data processed?
- What do peer-to-peer transport, end-to-end encryption, and self-hosting mean for each product?
- Which target audience would prefer Private Meet over an existing combination of tools?

## Evidence strategy

A delegated research track will discover candidates and read official pricing, security, privacy, feature, and self-hosting documentation. Separate organizer subscriptions from participant access, and monthly billing from annual-billing monthly equivalents. Record uncertain or sales-only pricing.

Check Glasser's catalog and pricing without running paid endpoints. Use existing free research facilities where they supply the same evidence. Paid requests require separate cost approval.

## Deliverables

- A linked wiki index.
- Categorized competitor matrix and pricing comparison.
- Privacy and deployment comparison.
- Positioning recommendations with current product limitations.

## Local scope

Private Meet currently combines booking links, two-person play-chip Texas Hold'em, peer-to-peer audio/video, and chat. Convex handles accounts and bookings; email and signalling also require services. No wallet, real-money payout, or blockchain transaction is part of this scope. TURN, calendar-provider synchronization, reconnect persistence, abuse controls, and real-device production verification remain gaps.

## Completion

Completed on 2026-10-10. The linked index, competitor comparison, privacy/trust analysis, and positioning/priorities pages are written. Review checked relative links, billing units, encryption-mode distinctions, cited evidence, and local product limitations. Exact Poker Now tier pricing and payer rules remain unresolved because the static extract lost headers and the live browser integration was unavailable. No paid Glasser endpoint was run.

## Boundaries

This is desk research, not a security audit or hands-on competitor test. A vendor's encryption statement is not evidence of independent security verification. Play chips and shared games should be compared separately from cash gambling.
